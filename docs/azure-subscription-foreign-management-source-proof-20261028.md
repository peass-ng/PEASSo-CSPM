# Subscription foreign-management exposure source proof

- The [Subscriptions List API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/rest/api/resources/subscriptions/list?view=rest-resources-2022-12-01) and [selected subscription GET](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/rest/api/resources/subscriptions/get?view=rest-resources-2022-12-01) return `tenantId` and `managedByTenants`. Microsoft defines each `ManagedByTenant.tenantId` as a tenant managing the subscription. The existing collector reads these fields through the per-scan subscription LIST or selected GET.
- [Azure role assignments](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/role-based-access-control/role-assignments) apply at management-group and subscription scopes and specify the principal, role, and scope. The existing collector lists subscription and tenant-root assignments and role definitions; Graph principal ownership and group membership are already available in the full scan.
- `peaso.analysis.internet_exposure._azure_subscription` compares managing tenants with the subscription owner and reuses the external RBAC join for direct subscription/root grants. It reports confirmed foreign tenants or principals as configured third-party access, preserves UNKNOWN when the manager list or RBAC/identity evidence is incomplete, and makes no claim of observed use or final effective permission after conditional/deny policies.
